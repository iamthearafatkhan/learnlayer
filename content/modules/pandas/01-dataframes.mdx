# Pandas for Data Analysis
Pandas is your programmable Excel. You will use it to load, clean, filter, and group datasets.

## 🎯 Core Concepts
- DataFrames and Series
- Reading data (`pd.read_csv`)
- Filtering (`df[df['age'] > 25]`)
- Grouping (`df.groupby()`)
- Handling missing data (`df.fillna()`)
