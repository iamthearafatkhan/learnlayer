# Seaborn
Seaborn is built on top of Matplotlib. It makes creating beautiful, complex statistical charts (like heatmaps and distributions) incredibly easy.

## 🎯 Core Concepts
- Distribution plots (`sns.histplot`)
- Categorical plots (`sns.barplot`, `sns.boxplot`)
- Correlation Heatmaps (`sns.heatmap`)
- Themes and styling
